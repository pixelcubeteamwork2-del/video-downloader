Questions Creators Ask

Will the files open in Premiere Pro?

Yes. With Edit-ready (H.264 + AAC) turned on (the default), every video is saved in a format Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut and CapCut open directly. YouTube's AV1/VP9 and TikTok's H.265 are converted for you.

Do TikTok downloads have a watermark?

No. The app saves TikTok's clean player copy, without the logo or username.

Why does a download sit at 0 B for a moment?

For HD YouTube, conversions, clips and MP3s the file is prepared first. It shows in your browser's downloads right away and finishes as soon as it's ready.

Can I download private or login-only posts?

Turn on Login in the menu bar to use your own browser login (or a cookies.txt file). It stays on your computer. Only download content you have the right to use.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It's free, there's no sign-up, and it runs on your own computer. See our Privacy Policy.
