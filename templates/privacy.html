Your privacy matters. In short: Video Downloader runs on your own computer, has no accounts and does not track you, and we don't collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no sign-up, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not send information about you to us or to any third party for tracking.

2. Links you paste and files you download

When you paste a link, the app contacts that website (for example YouTube or TikTok) to read the post and download the files you choose. This is the same kind of request your browser makes when you visit the page. Downloaded files are briefly stored in a temporary folder on your computer while they are prepared, and are deleted automatically afterwards. Finished downloads are saved by your browser to the folder you normally use for downloads.

3. Optional login (cookies)

Some posts are only visible to logged-in users. If you turn on Login, the app can use the login cookies from a browser you choose, or a cookies.txt file you upload. This setting is off by default.

  • Your cookies stay on your computer. They are only sent to the website they belong to, in order to load the posts you ask for.
  • An uploaded file is saved as cookies.txt, and your browser choice as settings.json, in the app's folder. Remove them at any time with the Remove button or by turning Login off.
  • Treat these files like a password and don't share them.

4. Information stored in your browser

The page remembers small preferences, such as whether "For video editors (H.264)" is turned on, using your browser's local storage. This stays in your browser and can be cleared with your browser's settings.

5. Third-party websites and services

The sites you download from (such as Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, LinkedIn and Pinterest) have their own privacy policies, which apply when the app connects to them. The page also loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of a normal web request. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

6. Error logs

If a download fails unexpectedly, technical details (such as the link and the error message) are written to logs/errors.log in the app's folder to help fix the problem. This file stays on your computer and you can delete it at any time.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.
